Following Gap Greater China's acquisition by Baozun, I led the IT PMO for the transition from global to local enterprise systems.

The work covered release readiness and integration across ERP, SCM, OMS, WMS, POS, CRM and other systems, coordinating business, technology and vendor teams. I also served as a supply chain IT liaison and led retail and inventory dashboard development.

The transition was completed three months ahead of schedule, with zero critical incidents during the Double 11 peak shopping season.
