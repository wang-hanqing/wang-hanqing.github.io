The idea began with a real accessibility barrier I observed while supporting a student learning Python with a screen reader: interruptions and changing focus could make it difficult to stay oriented.

Our seven-person interdisciplinary team developed CodeCompass, a proposed guidance mode that helps users understand where they are, what just happened and what to do next. I led the team and contributed to problem discovery and product concept development.

The proposal won first place at the Microsoft Product Vision Pitch Night in November 2026. It is a product proposal, not an implemented or released Microsoft feature.
