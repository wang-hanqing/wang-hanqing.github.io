The challenge was to shift from project-based delivery to a product operating model, changing how digital initiatives were organized, funded and evaluated.

As Project Delivery Lead and Digital PMO, I helped establish a cross-functional digital hub of approximately 50 people across three workstreams. We introduced product owner roles, product-based budgeting and outcome metrics, working with Product Managers and Product Owners on agile delivery and executive governance.

The hub delivered 90+ digital products in its first year. The work received recognition through the Accenture China GTC Award.
