# Website 2.0
## Purpose
Professional-first personal website and living archive of work, ideas and interests. Not a resume replica or student portfolio. English-first.
## Architecture
Home, About, Work, Journal, Beyond Work, Contact. Contact links in shared footer. Keep existing GitHub Pages URL. Jekyll, Markdown/YAML, plain CSS and minimal vanilla JS.
## Visual system
Soft Digital Editorial, informed by information design rather than imitation of an exhibition. Preserve white #FFFFFF, soft blue #EEF3FB, navy #16325C, link blue #2456C9, secondary text #5B616C. Inter for headings/body/navigation, IBM Plex Mono for numbers/dates/metadata. Natural hierarchy through whitespace and typography. Avoid decorative numbering, excessive headings, heavy shadows, gradients, dashboards, cold futuristic styling. Use real photos and screenshots, not stock illustrations presented as actual work.
## Home
Hero: Making sense of complexity. Strategy, digital transformation & analytics. 14+ years across consulting and corporate leadership; curiosity about people and culture. No portrait on Home. Six featured Work entries, balanced three professional and three projects: Accenture, Gap, Shiseido, CodeCompass, CollegeFit, Draw Your Mood. Professional entries appear early. Latest three published Journal entries. Photo-led Beyond Work preview.
## About
Natural portrait when supplied; narrative of professional journey rather than chronological timeline. Three shared-band metrics: 14+ years, 90+ digital products delivered by hub in first year, 15+ enterprise systems integrated. Concise Columbia and Fudan education, PSM II/PSPO I, Mandarin/English/Korean. No career timeline chart. LinkedIn and resume links.
## Work
One index; two categories only: Professional Work (#DCE7FB, text #16325C) and Projects (#EAEAF2, text #4C506D). All / Professional Work / Projects filter; explicit text labels as well as color. Professional cases include context, role, approach and outcomes; projects include problem, method, contribution, results and reflections. No forced headings. Clearly distinguish proposals, prototypes, research and implemented solutions. No confidential employer data or student records.
Initial candidates: Accenture digital hub, Gap integration, Shiseido strategy, IRC M&E, CodeCompass (award-winning proposal, not released feature), CollegeFit (in development), Draw Your Mood, Reddit retention research. Later: child poverty visualization, PopFlow, DealLens.
## Journal
Three categories: Insights, Updates, Field Notes. Reverse chronological index, short summaries, optional images and LinkedIn/related work links. Markdown publication with automatic index and homepage latest entries. Candidate entries: CodeCompass award, Ethical AI CoLab, NYT product management, Korean fan dance, MoMA information design, GLASS talk. These are editorial plans, not published posts; obtain or review actual text before publishing. No invented URLs or dates.
## Beyond Work
Photo-led, multiple photos per album, minimal text. Arts & Culture, Community & Connection, Places & Exploration. Fan dance, museums, campus community, hiking. Optional Journal cross-links. Real photos only, permission-aware, no private student information. Responsive gallery, descriptive alt text, lazy loading, keyboard-friendly photo access, no autoplay.
## Acceptance
Target WCAG 2.2 AA, not a certification claim. Semantic headings/landmarks, skip link, keyboard navigation, visible focus, color independence, ordinary text contrast >=4.5:1, large text >=3:1, reduced motion, accessible controls. Responsive at mobile and desktop widths. Verify Chrome/Safari and VoiceOver/NVDA, Lighthouse/axe, broken links and build output. Use explicit image dimensions where available.
## Deployment
Develop on website-v2, keep main intact until user reviews. Do not merge or change Pages publishing settings before approval. Missing assets and unreviewed articles stay unpublished. Review existing resume before launch. Document how to add projects, posts and photo albums.
